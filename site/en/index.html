What we build

Four solutions to everyday problems.

Everything ships with its automated tests: an agent checks every change before it reaches your customers.

  • 01

    The problem

    People message you on WhatsApp all day and you answer by hand, always the same things.

    What we do

    We build an agent that replies, books the appointments and, when a question is not for it, passes it to a person.

  • 02

    The problem

    Your sales live in a spreadsheet only one person understands, and getting a number means waiting.

    What we do

    We build a dashboard you ask in plain words, "how much did we sell in September?", and it answers with the number.

  • 03

    The problem

    Your website is old and on a phone it looks bad or does not load at all.

    What we do

    We build you a new site, fast and good on any screen, tested before it goes live.

  • 04

    The problem

    You already have a chatbot or an app, but it fails or says whatever and nobody knows why.

    What we do

    We review it, test it and tell you what is wrong and how to fix it.

Origin

Žustro: quick, agile, sharp.

The name

In Croatian, žustro means quick and full of energy. It also sounds like Žustović, the surname of an ancestor of Jeremías who left Plomin, a village in Istria, for Rosario in the nineteenth century.

In March 2027 the studio moves to Rijeka, forty minutes from Plomin. The journey the family made, in reverse.

The checkerboard

The Z in the logo is 13 squares of a 5 by 5 board. As on Croatia's coat of arms, the first square is always red. And each square is a passing test: hence the motto.
